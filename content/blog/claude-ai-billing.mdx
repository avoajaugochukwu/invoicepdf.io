Claude AI billing is a recurring subscription: you pick a plan, Anthropic charges your card on a set cycle, and you get an invoice you can download. There's a free tier, and paid plans run from about **$20/month for Pro** up to **$200/month for Max**, with Team seats in between. Business API usage is billed separately, by the token. That's the whole model in one breath.

The two things people actually come here for are: *what does it cost*, and *how do I get the receipt for my expense report*. This guide answers both, plus how the billing cycle works, the taxes question, and the mix-ups that trip people up. If you're here because you need to bill *your own* clients—not just expense Claude—our [free invoice generator](/invoice-generator) makes a clean, downloadable PDF in a couple of minutes.

An [invoice](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/i/invoice.asp) is just an itemized record of what you paid, for what, and when. Claude produces one for every charge. Let's start with what those charges are.

---

## Claude AI plans and what they cost

Anthropic sells Claude in a few tiers. According to [Anthropic's pricing page](https://www.anthropic.com/pricing), the individual and team plans break down roughly like this (prices are in USD and change over time—confirm the current numbers before you buy):

| Plan | Price | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Casual use, limited daily messages |
| Pro | ~$20/month (about $17/month billed annually) | Individuals who hit the free limits |
| Max 5x | ~$100/month | Heavy individual users; 5× Pro's usage |
| Max 20x | ~$200/month | Power users; 20× Pro's usage |
| Team | ~$25–$30/seat/month | Small teams; annual is cheaper than monthly |

A few things worth knowing before you pick:

- **Max is more usage, not a smarter model.** The Max tiers give you a bigger usage bucket (5× or 20× Pro's limits), not a different Claude. If you keep hitting caps, Max buys headroom; if you don't, Pro is plenty.
- **Annual billing is cheaper on Pro and Team.** Paying yearly on Pro drops the effective monthly price by roughly 15%. Max, per Anthropic, is billed monthly only.
- **Team has a per-seat, per-month structure.** You're billed for the number of seats, and the annual rate (about $25/seat) beats month-to-month (about $30/seat).
- **The API is a different animal.** If you're a developer, API access is billed by usage—per million tokens in and out—not as a flat subscription. More on that split below.

## How Claude billing actually works

Once you're on a paid plan, the mechanics are straightforward:

**It renews automatically on your anniversary date.** A monthly plan charges every month on the day you subscribed; an annual plan charges once a year. There's no invoice "due date" to chase—the card on file is charged on renewal. If you want to change the billing date, Anthropic's [billing FAQ](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8325618-paid-plan-billing-faqs) notes there's no direct switch; you cancel and resubscribe on the date you want.

**Payment is by card.** Web subscriptions take credit or debit cards only—no PayPal, no bank transfer for the web plan. If you subscribed through the iOS or Android app, that charge runs through Apple or Google's payment system instead, and you manage it there.

**Tax is added at checkout.** Any applicable tax is calculated from your billing location and itemized on the invoice. In the US that means **sales tax**, and whether it applies depends on your state—many digital services are taxed, some aren't. VAT is a non-US concept and won't appear on a US invoice. This is general information, not tax advice; your invoice shows the exact line.

**The charge shows up as Anthropic.** Anthropic is the company that makes Claude, so your bank or card statement typically shows an Anthropic or Claude.ai descriptor. If a charge looks unfamiliar, match its amount and date against your invoice before disputing it.

**Finalized invoices can't be edited.** Once an invoice is paid, its details are locked—you can't change the business name or add a tax ID after the fact. Set those *before* you pay (see the next section).

## How to find and download your Claude invoice

This is the part most pricing articles skip. Here's the exact path for each type of account.

**For a Pro or Max subscription:**

1. Open Claude and click your **initials or name** in the lower-left corner.
2. Choose **Settings**, then **Billing**.
3. Find the **Invoices** section.
4. Click **View** next to the month you need, then download the PDF.

Every invoice is also emailed to your billing address automatically. If you can't find one, search your inbox for the subject line **"Your receipt from Anthropic"**—the receipt email is the fastest way to locate a past charge.

**For API or Console (developer) payments:**

1. Sign in to the developer **Console** with a Billing or Admin role.
2. Go to **Console Settings**, then **Billing**.
3. Open the **Invoice history** section.
4. Click **View** or **Download** next to the invoice. Anthropic's help page on [finding API and Console invoices](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/10366473-where-can-i-find-full-receipts-and-invoices-for-my-claude-api-and-console-payments) notes that **View** opens a Stripe tab with extra download options.

**Set the right details first.** When you add or update your card in Settings, then Billing, you can tick **"Use a different name on invoices"** to put your business name (rather than your personal name) on every future invoice. Do this *before* the next renewal—remember, paid invoices can't be edited afterward.

## Worked example 1: monthly vs annual Pro

Say you're a freelancer weighing Pro. Monthly, that's about **$20 × 12 = $240 a year**. Annual billing runs roughly $17/month, charged once as **~$204 for the year**. The difference is about **$36 a year**—the ~15% annual discount in real dollars.

The trade-off: annual is one larger charge on your card up front, and if you cancel mid-year you've already paid for the whole term. If you're confident you'll use Claude for twelve months, annual wins. If you're trialing it, month-to-month keeps you flexible. Either way, the invoice in Settings, then Billing shows exactly what you were charged and when.

## Worked example 2: expensing Claude for your business

Now say you run a one-person consulting shop and use Claude Pro for client work. Here's the clean way to handle it at tax time:

1. **Pay with your business card**, and set your business name on the invoice (Settings → Billing → "Use a different name on invoices").
2. **Download the invoice** each month, or keep the "Your receipt from Anthropic" emails in a labeled folder.
3. **Log it as a software expense** in your bookkeeping.

A software subscription used for your business is generally a [deductible business expense](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/deducting-business-expenses) in the US—but that depends on your circumstances, so treat this as general information, not tax advice. The reason to save the PDF isn't just the deduction: the IRS expects self-employed people to [keep supporting documents](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/recordkeeping) for what they report. A downloaded invoice is that document. A bank line that just says "Anthropic" is not.

## Subscription billing vs API billing

These get confused constantly, so it's worth being explicit:

- **Subscription (Pro / Max / Team):** a flat recurring fee to use the Claude app and its features. One invoice per billing cycle. Managed in **Settings → Billing** in Claude.
- **API (developer):** usage-based billing—you pay per token for what your applications send and receive. Invoiced through the **Console**, not the app. This is what powers custom tools and integrations, and the cost scales with how much you use it.

They're separate accounts with separate invoices. Your Pro receipt won't include API charges, and your API invoice won't mention your Pro plan. If you're doing both—say, a Pro plan for yourself and API access for a product you're building—expect two sets of invoices in two places.

## Common Claude billing mistakes

- **Forgetting to set the business name before paying.** Finalized invoices can't be edited. Set the name (and any details you need) in Settings, then Billing *before* the renewal, not after.
- **Looking for the invoice in your email spam folder and giving up.** It's always in **Settings → Billing** too, even if the email got filtered. The app is the source of truth.
- **Mixing up the app charge and the API charge.** If a charge doesn't match your subscription price, check whether it's an API bill in the Console before assuming something's wrong.
- **Paying annually and then canceling in month two.** The annual term is paid up front. Only commit to annual if you're confident about the year.
- **Not saving invoices for expenses.** A card statement isn't a receipt. Download the PDF (or keep the email) so you have a real record if you deduct the cost.
- **Assuming Max means a better model.** It's a bigger usage allowance, not a smarter Claude. Don't overpay for capability you're not getting.

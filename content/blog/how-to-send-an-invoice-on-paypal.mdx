If you’re a freelancer, small business owner, or side hustler, getting paid quickly and securely is a top priority. That’s where **PayPal invoices** come in. They’re professional, easy to set up, and make it simple for clients worldwide to pay you.


In this guide, I’ll walk you through **how to send an invoice on PayPal**, step by step, plus share common mistakes to avoid, and answer FAQs so you can invoice with confidence.


---


## Why Use PayPal Invoices?


Before diving in, let’s talk about why PayPal invoices are so popular:

- **Professional look** – Your invoices look polished, not like a quick text message.
- **Global reach** – Accept payments from almost anywhere in the world.
- **Multiple payment options** – Clients can pay with a card, PayPal balance, or bank account.
- **Tracking made easy** – You’ll always know which invoices are paid and which are pending.

If you’ve been manually requesting payments, upgrading to invoices will save you time and help you look more professional.


---


## Step-by-Step: How to Send an Invoice on PayPal


### 1. Log in to Your PayPal Account


Go to [PayPal.com](https://www.paypal.com/) and sign in with your account. Make sure your account is verified before sending invoices.


### 2. Go to the “Invoicing” Section

- On desktop: Click **Tools** > **Invoicing**
- On mobile app: Tap **More** > **Create Invoice**

### 3. Create a New Invoice


Click **Create Invoice**. You’ll see a form with details to fill in.


### 4. Add Client Information


Enter your client’s **name and email address**. Double-check the email to avoid sending it to the wrong person.


### 5. Fill in Invoice Details


Add:

- **Item or service description** (be clear and specific)
- **Quantity and rate**
- **Due date**

You can also add notes or payment terms, like “Payment due within 7 days.”


### 6. Review and Customize

- Add your **logo** for branding.
- Choose your **currency**.
- Apply taxes if necessary.

### 7. Send the Invoice


Once everything looks good, click **Send**. Your client will receive the invoice by email and can pay directly with one click.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid


Even though PayPal makes invoicing simple, beginners sometimes slip up. Here are mistakes to watch out for:

- ❌ **Not including clear descriptions** – Clients should know exactly what they’re paying for.
- ❌ **Forgetting due dates** – Without one, payments can drag on.
- ❌ **Using the wrong currency** – Always confirm your client’s preferred payment currency.
- ❌ **Skipping fees awareness** – PayPal charges a small fee per transaction; factor this into your pricing.

## FAQs About PayPal Invoicing


### 1. Does it cost money to send an invoice on PayPal?


Creating and sending invoices is free. PayPal only charges a fee when your client pays.


### 2. Can clients pay without a PayPal account?


Yes! Clients can pay with a credit or debit card, even if they don’t have a PayPal account.


### 3. Can I edit an invoice after sending it?


Yes, if the client hasn’t paid yet. Once payment is made, you’ll need to issue a new invoice or refund.


### 4. How long does it take to receive money?


Payments are usually instant, but sometimes it may take 1–3 business days depending on the client’s payment method.

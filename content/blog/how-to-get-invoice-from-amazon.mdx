You bought something on Amazon and now you need the invoice — for an expense report, a reimbursement, tax records, or a warranty claim. Here is exactly how to get it, for every scenario.

---

## The standard flow: personal Amazon order invoice

This works for most orders fulfilled by Amazon directly.

**Step 1 — Log in and click "Returns & Orders."** That button sits in the top-right corner of any Amazon page, next to your cart icon.

**Step 2 — Find the order.** Your orders are listed newest-first. Use the search bar or the date filter (drop-down on the right) to narrow down if you have a long history.

**Step 3 — Click "Invoice."** On most orders you will see an "Invoice" link directly in the order row. Click it.

**Step 4 — If no "Invoice" link shows, click "Order Details."** That opens the full order summary. Scroll down to the "Order Summary" or "Payment Information" block — the invoice link is usually there.

**Step 5 — The invoice opens as a PDF in a new browser tab.** From there you can save it (File → Save) or print it (see the printing section below).

> The IRS recommends keeping receipts and invoices for all business expenses. See [What kinds of records should I keep?](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/what-kind-of-records-should-i-keep) on IRS.gov for the full guidance on how long to retain them.

---

## How to download and save an Amazon invoice as a PDF

Once the invoice is open in your browser:

- **Chrome / Edge:** Press Ctrl+P (Windows) or Cmd+P (Mac), set the destination to "Save as PDF," and click Save.
- **Firefox:** File → Print → PDF drop-down → Save to PDF.
- **Safari:** File → Print → PDF button (bottom-left of the print dialog) → Save as PDF.

Name the file with the order number and date (e.g., `amazon-invoice-112-3456789-2026.pdf`) so it is easy to find later. Keep a folder organized by month or year if you buy from Amazon regularly for business purposes.

## Amazon Business: invoices for business purchases

If your organization uses Amazon Business, the invoicing flow is more powerful.

**Business invoices are generated automatically** for qualifying purchases. To access them:

1. Log into your Amazon Business account.
2. Go to **Business Orders** (a separate tab from personal orders once you are in Your Orders).
3. Find the order and click **Invoice** — same location as personal orders.

**Additional Amazon Business features worth knowing:**

| Feature | Where to find it |
|---|---|
| Bulk invoice export | Business Analytics → Export → Invoice history |
| Tax-exempt purchasing | Account Settings → Tax Exemption |
| Approval workflows | Business Settings → Purchasing Policies |
| Consolidated invoices | Some enrolled organizations receive monthly consolidated invoices by email |

If your company is not yet on Amazon Business and you buy frequently for work, it is worth enrolling — the reporting and invoice-management tools are significantly better than a standard account.

## Getting an invoice from a third-party seller

Not every Amazon order ships from Amazon's own warehouse. Third-party sellers list and fulfill many products, and their invoicing works differently.

**Check first — many third-party invoices are already there.** Follow the standard Returns & Orders flow. If the seller integrated their system with Amazon's invoicing, you will see a downloadable invoice just like an Amazon-fulfilled order.

**If no invoice appears, contact the seller directly:**

1. Go to **Your Orders** and click the order.
2. Click **"Contact Seller."**
3. Write a short message: "Hi, could you please send me an invoice for order [order number]? I need it for [expense report / reimbursement / warranty]. Thank you."
4. The seller typically responds by email within 24–48 hours.

Most sellers are happy to provide one — it costs them nothing and it is a normal business request. If a seller does not respond within a few days, escalate via Amazon Customer Service with your order ID.

**A note on VAT invoices:** If you are shopping on a non-US Amazon marketplace (Amazon.co.uk, Amazon.de, Amazon.fr, etc.), those platforms operate under VAT regimes and sellers are often required to provide a VAT invoice. That requirement does not apply to US Amazon purchases, which are governed by state and local sales tax rules instead.

## Digital purchases: Kindle, Prime Video, apps

Digital orders follow the same general path with one extra filter step:

1. Go to **Your Orders**.
2. Click the **"Digital Orders"** filter tab (or select it from the filter drop-down).
3. Find the purchase and click **Invoice** or **Order Details**.

For Prime Video rentals and Kindle purchases the document is more of a purchase confirmation than a traditional invoice, but it shows the item, price, and date — enough for most reimbursement or expense purposes.

## Troubleshooting: what to do when no invoice is available

**Invoice not showing up yet?** Invoices on some orders are generated only after the item is marked as delivered, not when you place the order. Wait 24–48 hours after delivery.

**"Invoice" link is greyed out or missing entirely.** This happens most often with:
- Subscribe & Save orders (the invoice appears after the subscription cycle completes)
- Pre-orders (invoice generates when the item ships)
- Some digital purchases processed through a third-party app store

**Invoice shows but the numbers look wrong.** Compare with the "Order Summary" section on your order details page. If there is a discrepancy after a return or partial refund, the invoice may not yet reflect the adjustment — check again in a day or two, or contact Amazon Customer Service.

**Bulk download needed.** Amazon does not offer a native one-click bulk download for all invoices. The fastest workaround is to open each order individually and save the PDF. Amazon Business accounts have a bulk export option in Business Analytics (see the table in the Amazon Business section above).

## Common mistakes

- **Confusing the order confirmation email with an invoice.** The confirmation email is not an invoice — it is a notification that Amazon received your order. The actual invoice is only available through the Returns & Orders page in your account.
- **Trying to use the packing slip as an invoice.** Packing slips often omit price details and do not carry seller tax information. Always pull the invoice from your account.
- **Not downloading before a return is completed.** Once a return is fully processed, the original invoice can be harder to locate. Download and save it before initiating a return.
- **Requesting from the wrong seller.** If an order has multiple items from different sellers, each item may have its own separate invoice. Check each line item.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does Amazon provide invoices?**

Yes. Amazon generates an invoice for most orders. Find it by going to Returns & Orders, selecting the order, and clicking Invoice on the order details page. Not every order type generates a downloadable invoice — digital purchases and some third-party seller orders may only show an order confirmation until you request one from the seller.

**How do I get a printable invoice from Amazon?**

Open the order in Returns & Orders, click Invoice, and when the invoice PDF opens in your browser, use your browser's print menu (Ctrl+P or Cmd+P) and choose Save as PDF or select your printer. The result is a clean, printable document showing the item, price, tax, and seller details.

**How do I get a tax invoice from an Amazon third-party seller?**

Go to Your Orders, find the order, and click Contact Seller. Ask the seller to send a formal invoice or receipt to your email. Most sellers respond within 24–48 hours. If the seller does not respond, contact Amazon Customer Service and reference the order ID.

**Can I get an invoice for a gift order?**

Gift orders intentionally omit price details from the packing slip, but the full invoice is still in your account. Go to Your Orders, find the order, and follow the standard steps — the invoice is visible only to the buyer, not the recipient, and it shows the full price breakdown.

**What should I do if no invoice is available for my Amazon order?**

First, wait 24–48 hours after delivery — invoices sometimes generate after the item ships or is marked delivered. If it still does not appear, message the seller via Contact Seller on the order page. As a last resort, contact Amazon Customer Service with your order ID and they can supply a transaction record.
